A smart tourism platform designed to make travel simpler, more affordable, and sustainable. It creates personalized itineraries based on traveler preferences, budget, available time, and destinations. The platform optimizes routes and expenses, promotes local businesses, highlights eco-friendly travel choices, and dynamically updates plans when weather, traffic, or other conditions change—helping travelers save time, reduce costs, and enjoy a seamless journey.

How the prototype works

This front-end prototype demonstrates the future smart tourism backend. In the production version, React would call a FastAPI backend, which would fetch tourism/weather/map data and run recommendation and optimization models.

  1. Collect tourist preferences.
  2. Score destinations and businesses.
  3. Optimize time, route and budget.
  4. Monitor weather/crowd/traffic.
  5. Re-plan when conditions change.
